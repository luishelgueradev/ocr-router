<objective>
Build the orchestration core: `runCascade` walks a profile's chain, applies the heuristic to decide "good enough," falls through on failure or low confidence, tracks best-so-far, enforces the bounds + one authoritative deadline, short-circuits a dead Ollama quota, and emits the full JOB-02 trace.

Purpose: This is the product's core value — automatic escalation that never loses the work. The heavy risk here is decision correctness (fall-through matrix) and abort propagation, both proven by a mocked-provider unit suite (no keys/network).
Output: `lib/v1/cascade/trace.js`, `lib/v1/cascade/runner.js`, and a green `test/cascade-runner.test.js`.
</objective>
